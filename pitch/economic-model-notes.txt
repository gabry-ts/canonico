All figures are assumptions to validate. None is measured. Horizon: year 2 run-rate.
Payload: pitch/economic-model.json (schemaVersion 1, EUR, integer cents).

WHAT WE SELL
- Paid pilot, 8 weeks, one process: EUR 6,000 one-off (project_fee).            [declared]
- Annual licence per company: EUR 15,000 / year (licence_year).                 [declared]
- Buyer: AI programme lead of a company rolling out an internal AI assistant.
  Co-buyers: process owners (operations, procurement), compliance.
- Assumption: 2 of 3 pilots convert to a licence.                                 [declared]

REVENUE (year 2)
  Pilots:    12 x 6,000   =  72,000  (one-off)
  Licences:   8 x 15,000  = 120,000  (recurring, ARR)
  Total                   = 192,000 EUR / year
  Check: 12 pilots x 2/3 = 8 licences.

CAC

  Per licensed customer:  9,300 x 1.5 (2/3 conv.) = 13,950 EUR
