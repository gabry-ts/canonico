# Office threads corpus (OIDA Benchmark Corpora)

Dataset `office-threads` from the OIDA Benchmark Corpora, companion data of the paper "Retrieval Is Not Enough: Why Organizational AI Needs Epistemic Infrastructure" by Federico Bottino, Carlo Ferrero, Nicholas Dosio and Pierfrancesco Beneventano.

Source: https://github.com/kakashi-ventures/oida-evaluation-corpora (corpora/office-threads)

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (see LICENSE). Files `corpus.jsonl`, `queries.jsonl` and `qrels/` are copied unchanged. The `raw/` sources are not included.

Used as the mocked chat source of the project `proj-office`.
