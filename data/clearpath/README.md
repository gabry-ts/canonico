# ClearPath corpus (OIDA Benchmark Corpora)

Dataset `org-consulting-clearpath` from the OIDA Benchmark Corpora, companion data of the paper "Retrieval Is Not Enough: Why Organizational AI Needs Epistemic Infrastructure" by Federico Bottino, Carlo Ferrero, Nicholas Dosio and Pierfrancesco Beneventano.

Source: https://github.com/kakashi-ventures/oida-evaluation-corpora (corpora/org-consulting-clearpath)
